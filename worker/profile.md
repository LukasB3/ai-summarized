The reader builds software with AI and follows the field closely.

Highest interest:
1. Models and releases: new models and model families from the major labs and from open-source groups; capabilities, benchmarks, pricing, context sizes, API changes.
2. Developer tools and agents: coding agents, agent frameworks, SDKs, APIs, MCP, evaluation and deployment tooling; how to build with AI.
3. Alignment and AI risk: alignment and interpretability research, safety evaluations, incidents, misuse, governance of frontier models.
4. Research on AI consciousness, sentience and model welfare.

Lower interest: funding rounds, executive moves, marketing partnerships, stock and market commentary, consumer gadgets, tech policy without a concrete AI rule.
